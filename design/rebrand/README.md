# Rebrand — sticker scrapbook in the sky (current)

Visual language adapted from a consumer map app's site and onboarding (sky photography, inflated chrome wordmark, lowercase copy, black pills, floating 3D objects, sticker cutouts). Name, imagery and copy are our own.

- Palette: `#FFFFFF` paper · `#B4D2F0` / `#9CC3EC` sky · `#0A0A0A` ink + pills · `#F7C5DD` sticker pink with `#E2549E` dashed edge · chrome only in the wordmark
- Type: Inter Tight, all lowercase. Bold, tight tracking for headlines (28–36px); medium for body
- Signature mechanic: every contributor is a cardboard standee placed where they stood. Tap a cutout to see the night from their photos.

## Onboarding (`onboarding/`)
1. `o1-splash` — chrome "open house" balloons in the sky, "step back inside.", get started
2. `o2-what-do-you-remember` — tap floating 3D objects for the kinds of nights you want
3. `o3-who-was-there` — friend cutout collage with sticker tags, sync contacts
4. `o4-where-was-it` — address + tilted place cards ("by @rohan · 3 nights"); interior only
5. `o5-let-us-see-the-night` — fanned prints, "38 found from oct 31", allow photos
6. `o6-building-your-house` — diorama assembling while photos fly in, progress

## App (`app/`)
1. `a1-home-the-house` — night chips, interior diorama with standees, room stickers, dock
2. `a2-room-cutouts` — standees placed in the real room, "4 people stood here", time slider
3. `a3-their-night` — one person's cutout + their photos, "stand where she stood"
4. `a4-your-nights` — website-style night cards, who's inside, add tonight

## Create a moment (`create-a-moment/`)
End-to-end flow: create → pick location → invite → photos matched by location + time → house builds → fast-forward → step inside. See its README.

## Step inside (`step-inside/`)
Walking through a finished moment: footprints at every photo spot, camera-true photos snapping into the 3D room, angles of one moment, who-stood-where map, and the motion spec.
